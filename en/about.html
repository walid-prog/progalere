Our story

Our story, in four chapters.

Chapter 01

Two worlds that didn't talk to each other enough.

One of the co-founders came from the world of auditing : years spent poring over the numbers of companies of every size, seeing firsthand what separated an SMB that thrives from one running out of breath. It was almost never a question of talent or effort. It was a question of wasted time : hours sunk into bookkeeping, manual processes that should have been automated long ago, decisions made blind for lack of up-to-date numbers.

Their tools :
QuickBooksSage 50ExcelPower AutomateMake
Chapter 02

The same problem, seen from another angle.

The other co-founder came from marketing, leading an agency that supported dozens of businesses on their positioning, campaigns, and image. The same observation, seen from another angle : businesses with excellent products or services that stayed invisible, or communicated without a clear direction, for lack of a coherent strategy.

Two areas of expertise, one same problem seen from two different angles. SMBs and self-employed workers juggle too many providers, too disconnected from each other, for a result that stays fragmented.

Their tools :
PositionnementCampagnesCRMContenu
Chapter 03

A simple decision : stop separating what should be together.

Rather than keep going their separate ways, the two co-founders chose to bring their expertise together under one roof. Not to stack on more services, but to offer what they themselves were looking for as professionals : a single point of contact who understands both the numbers and the brand, able to connect accounting, automation, strategy, and training into one coherent vision.

That's where the name Wavora comes from : the idea of a continuous movement, of waves coming together rather than silos ignoring each other.

To deliver on that vision with the same standard across every area of expertise, the two co-founders brought together a full team of specialists, able to cover every aspect of an engagement with the same rigor, from the first number analyzed to the last campaign launched.

Chapter 04

A vision that goes beyond the starting point.

Wavora started with four pillars : operational accounting, automation, strategy, and training. But the ambition doesn't stop there. As SMBs' needs evolve, with AI redefining what's possible for a small business, Wavora intends to expand what it offers, always with the same logic : less fragmentation, more clarity, a single trusted partner to help you grow.
