Privacy Policy

Wavora is committed to protecting the personal information of its visitors and clients, in accordance with the Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (Act 25, Québec).

Last updated : August 26, 2026

Person responsible for the protection of personal information

Walid Bour is responsible for the application of this policy. For any questions, write to bonjour@wavora.ca.

Information collected

When you book a consultation through our scheduling tool (Calendly), the following information may be collected : name, email address, phone number (if provided), and the details of the selected appointment.

Purpose of collection

This information is used solely to schedule, confirm, and follow up on your consultation with Wavora.

Transfer outside Québec

Calendly, our scheduling tool provider, is a company based in the United States. Your information may therefore be transferred and hosted outside Québec as part of using this service. Calendly applies its own security measures, described in its privacy policy.

Retention of information

We retain information related to your appointment only for as long as necessary to follow up on the consultation and any business relationship that may result from it.

Your rights

You may at any time request access to the information we hold about you, request that it be corrected, or withdraw your consent to its collection, by writing to bonjour@wavora.ca.

Complaints

If you believe your rights have not been respected, you may file a complaint with the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec (cai.gouv.qc.ca).
